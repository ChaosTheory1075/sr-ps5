PPSA54107/install/ - put your Saints Row and Saints Row 2 files here
==================================================================

- Saints Row: your disc image (.iso, any name), or a folder of the disc's
  files (default.xex and the rest), and its Title Update 1 package
  (TU_1A521UH_..., from 545107D1/000B0000/).
- Saints Row 2: your disc image (.iso, any name), or a folder of the disc's
  files, and its Title Update 3 package (TU_1A521VS_..., from
  545107FC/000B0000/).
  (One game is enough to play it; the app tells the two discs apart by
  itself. A game needs its title update: the programs are built from it.)
- DLC (optional): the packages from 545107D1/00000002/ (Saints Row) and
  545107FC/00000002/ (Saints Row 2), as many as you have.
- Saves (optional): an Xbox 360 save package, copied off the 360's drive
  (Content/<profile>/545107D1/00000001/<save> for Saints Row,
  .../545107FC/00000001/<save> for Saints Row 2). Import it from the SAVES
  page.

The packages have no extension; that's normal. The app tells what each file is
from what's in it, whatever it's called. Extract any .7z, .rar or .zip on a PC
first.

Open the app: it installs the discs, the title updates and the DLC by itself,
one at a time, then asks whether to delete the files it installed from (X:
delete, O: keep; kept files aren't installed again).
