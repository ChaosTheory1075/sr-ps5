PPSA54107/install/ - put your Saints Row and Saints Row 2 files here
==================================================================

- Saints Row: your disc image (.iso, any name), or a folder of the disc's
  files (default.xex and the rest).
- Saints Row 2: your disc image (.iso, any name), or a folder of the disc's
  files.
  (One game is enough to play it; the app tells the two discs apart by
  itself. No title updates are needed.)
- Saints Row 2's DLC (optional): the packages from 415608A7/00000002/ (names
  like 15C18C48011FEE9CB311DF713653DF3BBA24B6DB41), as many as you have.
- HD textures (optional): the PC 4K texture packs from Nexus Mods - Allagga's
  "Saints Row RE" (Saints Row) and Warcaco's pack (Saints Row 2). Extract the
  downloads on a PC and copy the folders in as they are: the app installs
  the textures from their .tpf files (the rest - ReShade's files - isn't
  used).
- Saves (optional): an Xbox 360 save package, copied off the 360's drive
  (Content/<profile>/4156084E/00000001/<save> for Saints Row,
  .../415608A7/00000001/<save> for Saints Row 2). Import it from the SAVES
  page.

The packages have no extension; that's normal. The app tells what each file is
from what's in it, whatever it's called. Extract any .7z, .rar or .zip on a PC
first.

Open the app: it installs the discs, the DLC and the textures by itself, one at a time, then
asks whether to delete the files it installed from (X: delete, O: keep; kept
files aren't installed again).
